Chemistry Interactive Labs

Every lab opens on a simulation you have to predict before you can run: the controls stay locked until you commit, a wrong answer is diagnosed by its cause rather than marked wrong, and the worked support fades from example to completion to independent practice. Each one follows Introduction → Simulation → Calculations → Exam practice → Recap & misconceptions, in HKEAA mark-scheme wording, with tap-to-flip misconception cards and collapsible teacher notes. The topic code in each header is the HKDSE unit it serves. Open any file directly in a browser: no network, no build, no account.

One design system across all 19 labs: _build/src/theme.css and tailwind.config.js, rebuilt with node _build/src/build.cjs and checked on phone, iPad and laptop viewports by node _build/src/qa.cjs.

Structure and bonding · Topic II

Topic II (e)–(g), (i)

Ionic & Covalent Bonding

Dot-and-cross with Bohr 2,8,8 shells — electron transfer (NaCl, MgO…) vs shared pairs (Cl₂…NH₃), dative NH₄⁺, structure→properties.

 Topic II (h)–(i)

Giant Covalent Structures — Diamond, Graphite, Graphene

Same element, same bond, opposite materials. Build the bonds one at a time and watch the fourth electron become delocalised; heat / conduct / cut probes; three failure states to diagnose; CO2 vs SiO2 full mark scheme. Covers graphene plus the applications requires.

 Topic VII (d)

Acids, bases, salts and analysis · Topics I, IV, XV

Topic IV (b)–(c)

Ionisation of Acids & Bases

Strong → vs weak ⇌ at true α (0.1 M CH₃COOH ≈ 1.3 %, pH 2.89), dilution shifts the equilibrium live, conductivity bulb, Ba(OH)₂ ×2 trap.

 Topic IV (e)–(f) + XV (c)

Acid–Base Titration

Burette to 0.05 cm³, concordant-set mean (not mean-of-all), wet-apparatus reasoning, H₂SO₄ 1:2 stoichiometry, standard-solution prep.

 Topic I (b)–(c) + IV (d)

Obtaining Salt from Seawater

Filter→evaporate with real solubility physics, the boil-to-dryness failure mode (spatter scored in loss account), cooling-crystallisation contrast, ppm & constant-mass calcs.

 Topic XV (a)

Flame Tests

The full mark-scheme procedure as a state machine — wire choice, conc-HCl, non-luminous mask, cobalt glass over Na bleed; Unknowns mode rejects charge-less answers.

Carbon compounds · Topics V, XI

Topic V (a) + (c)

Crude Oil & Fractional Distillation

Particles condense where column T falls to their b.p. (no random heights); Cracking mode splits C₁₀H₂₂ over Al₂O₃ — separation vs chemical change.

 Topic XI (b)

Enantiomers in 3D

Drag-rotate real mirror pairs, superimpose slider scores n/4 groups aligned (chiral never hits 4/4), racemate & HCN-addition panel.
How to study with a lab: (1) read the Introduction and answer the hook question before you are shown anything — the commitment is what makes the rest stick; (2) in the Simulation, lock in every prediction first: the good labs keep the controls disabled until you do; (3) break the deliberate failure states and read what actually changed; (4) in Calculations, do the worked example, then the completion problem, then the independent one — the fading support is the point; (5) mark your own written answer against the revealed mark scheme and be honest; (6) in Recap, tick every goal you can do without notes and every mark loss you have actually made.
