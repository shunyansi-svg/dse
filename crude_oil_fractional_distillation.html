Down the column: b.p. ↑ · viscosity ↑ · flammability ↓ · volatility ↓ · chain length ↑ — due to stronger intermolecular (London) forces on longer chains. Boiling overcomes intermolecular forces only; no covalent C–C bonds break — that is why this is a physical change. Particle colour codes the band (real fractions run colourless → dark brown; bitumen drains slowly).
