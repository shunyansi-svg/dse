Teacher notes

Myth

H₂ always forms at the cathode of any aqueous solution.

Tap to flip

Fact

Only if the metal is MORE reactive than hydrogen. Cu²⁺ and Ag⁺ are reduced more readily than H⁺ — a metal deposit forms instead.

Myth

Dilute and concentrated NaCl contain different ions, so they must give different products for that reason.

Tap to flip

Fact

The INVENTORY is identical (Na⁺, Cl⁻, H⁺, OH⁻). Concentration changes which species is oxidised MORE READILY: dilute → O₂, concentrated → Cl₂.

Myth

Solid NaCl can be electrolysed.

Tap to flip

Fact

No — the ions are fixed in the lattice and cannot move. It must be molten or aqueous so ions are free to migrate to the electrodes.
