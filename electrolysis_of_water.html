Teacher notes

Myth?

Electrons flow through the water from anode to cathode.

Fact

Electrons travel only in the metal wires and electrodes. In solution the charge is carried by IONS: H⁺ migrates to the cathode, SO₄²⁻/OH⁻ to the anode. (Switch on both overlays in the sim and watch.)

Myth?

Because water is H₂O, the gases must come off 1 : 1.

Fact

The formula is not the stoichiometry of discharge. Balancing electrons across the two half-equations gives 2H₂O(l) → 2H₂(g) + O₂(g): a 2 : 1 volume ratio, not 1 : 1.

Myth?

The sulfuric acid is used up as electrolysis proceeds.

Fact

The H⁺ discharged at the cathode is fully regenerated at the anode — the acid is a spectator electrolyte. Only water is consumed, so the acid slowly becomes more concentrated.
