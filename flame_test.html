Task 3Explain the colour — the 3-mark chain

Try writing the explanation first, then climb down the hint ladder as far as you need. Each mark needs a named electron, a direction, and a photon.

Where the colour comes from

Energy from the flame promotes (excites) electrons to higher energy levels → the electrons fall back to lower levels / the ground state → they emit (visible) photons. Each metal ion has a different set of energy gaps, so a different frequency (wavelength) of light is emitted — a different colour. Mg²⁺ shows nothing because its transitions release energy outside the visible spectrum ("no transitions" earns nothing). The 3-mark chain is heat → electrons excited → fall back → emit photons; "atoms/molecules gain energy" without mentioning electrons, or light being "absorbed", scores 0–1.

Beyond this lab: those specific frequencies are the element's line-emission spectrum — the quantitative side (ΔE = hν) is beyond this level.
