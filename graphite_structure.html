Myth

Graphite melts easily because it is soft.

Tap to flip

Fact

Softness = layers SLIDING on weak vdW forces. Melting/subliming breaks the STRONG covalent bonds within layers — graphite does not melt at 1 atm; it sublimes ≈ 3600–3700 °C. Softness and mp answer different questions.

Myth

Interlayer attraction is covalent or metallic — a sea of electrons around positive ions.

Tap to flip

Fact

Between layers there is only van der Waals attraction. Metallic wording earns 0: graphite is not metallically bonded, and an element has no ions — carriers are delocalised electrons.

Myth

All four outer electrons of each carbon bond, so diamond and graphite conduct the same.

Tap to flip

Fact

In graphite each C forms 3 covalent bonds; the 4th electron is delocalised and mobile along the layers → conduction. Diamond uses all 4 electrons in bonds: no mobile charges, no conduction.
