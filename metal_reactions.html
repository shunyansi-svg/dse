Teacher notes

Wrong idea

Magnesium + steam gives magnesium hydroxide, Mg(OH)₂.

Tap to flip

Mark scheme

Steam gives the OXIDE: Mg(s) + H₂O(g) → MgO(s) + H₂(g). Only cold water gives the (sparingly soluble) hydroxide — a known a past paper/13 trap.

Wrong idea

Reactivity falls down Group 1 because the atoms get bigger and “need more energy”.

Tap to flip

Mark scheme

Reactivity INCREASES down the group: extra shells → outer e⁻ further from the nucleus + more shielding → weaker attraction → the electron is lost more readily (Li < Na < K).

Wrong idea

Metal + dilute HCl gives a salt and hydrogen — so metal oxide + dilute HCl also gives hydrogen.

Tap to flip

Mark scheme

Oxide/hydroxide + acid is neutralisation: salt + water ONLY, no gas (CuO-type logic): MgO(s) + 2HCl(aq) → MgCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l). Metal + acid is redox; oxide + acid is not.
