Molar volume at r.t.p. — the Topic IX (c) value

One mole of any gas occupies 24 dm³ at room temperature and pressure. This value belongs to Topic IX (c); Topic III (c) prepares you for it by giving you the mole. Use it in both directions: V = n × 24 and n = V ÷ 24.
